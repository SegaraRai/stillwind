When a physical utility conflicts with a logical utility, such as `px-2 pt-2`, the `conflicting-utilities` finding shows how to use logical properties for both: it names a logical utility from your design system that writes the same value, such as `pbs-2` for `pt-2`. The suggestion uses the sides of horizontal left-to-right writing modes and does not recommend logical properties over physical ones.
