<CompilerError code="SW90003" category="internal" />

This error occurs when the compiler's record of your stylesheet imports does not match the stylesheets that it loaded. It often follows a change to an `@import`.

## How to report

[Open an issue](https://github.com/SegaraRai/stillwind/issues) with the error
message and:

- Your stylesheet and the files that it imports
- Whether you had just added, removed, or changed an `@import`
- The version of the Stillwind packages
